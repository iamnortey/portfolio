# Prepnest Library: structured teaching resources

**Status:** protected review build. Public launch and educator acceptance are not claimed.

[Portfolio](../README.md)

## The problem and approach

Teachers need to find relevant material, understand its context and prepare it for a lesson. Prepnest Library brings curriculum browsing, resource discovery, past-question navigation and editorial preparation into a structured product.

I have worked on the interface, content organization and preparation workflows using AI-assisted development. A November 2026 hosted review build passed its build and TypeScript checks. Product acceptance, academic review and a complete sign-in journey remain separate checks.

The work is designed for its Ghanaian education context. It is not presented as a US school deployment or as independently validated teaching material.

## Transferable experience

The same product skills can help a small business organize staff procedures, search a knowledge collection and maintain useful documents. The shared problem is making information easy to find, understand and keep current.

Prepnest Library is distinct from the [Prepnest mobile study project](./prepnest.md). Their architectures and release states should not be combined.
