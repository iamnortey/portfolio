# GHSchools: discovery and preparation for families

**Status:** protected review build; not a public admissions service.

[Portfolio](../README.md)

## The problem and approach

School discovery requires more than a list of names. Families need a way to explore options, compare information and prepare the details they will need next.

I am building GHSchools around search, school profiles, comparison and application preparation. The review build includes connected family-facing flows and responsive interface work. Hosted review checks covered authenticated access to the preview and multiple screen widths; they do not establish production family accounts or durable submissions to schools.

## Transferable experience

The work develops skills useful to independent service businesses: directories, comparison tools, structured intake, clear multi-step forms and review before submission.

I preserve the project’s Ghanaian school context. Catalogue scale, school relationships, admissions outcomes and institutional verification are not claimed as established results.
